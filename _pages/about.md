## Call for Presentations

We welcome talks on programming languages research (broadly construed) from all career stages, including work in progress. No full paper is required.
We tentatively plan for 20 minutes per talk, and talks will be in person only.

> **Submission deadline:** Sunday, November 15, 2026 (AoE)
>
> **Speaker notification:** Wednesday, November 18, 2026 (planned)
>
> **Presentations:** Tuesday, December 1, 2026 (in person)

Please submit one response per proposed talk.
